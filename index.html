About Us

GERO Logistics is a Canadian-based logistics startup dedicated to connecting businesses and individuals with reliable transport, forwarding, and delivery services. Based in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario, we provide seamless coordination between shippers, carriers, and customers. Our mission is simple — to bring logistics closer to your neighborhood and shipments to your doorstep.

We collaborate with trusted partners across Canada and internationally to ensure each shipment is handled safely, efficiently, and with full transparency.

Our Services

  • Wholesale Trade: We help connect bulk suppliers with buyers, providing support for transport coordination and trade logistics.
  • Local Pickup & Delivery: We coordinate local pickups and deliveries within Sault Ste. Marie and nearby regions with licensed drivers.
  • Freight Forwarding Support: Assistance with domestic and international freight forwarding through trusted logistics partners.
  • Partnered International Shipping: Global shipping coordination in collaboration with our international partners for smooth customs and documentation handling.
  • Freight Management Support: Scheduling, route coordination, and cargo management for reliable shipping outcomes.
  • Freight Management Tracking: Real-time updates and communication to keep clients informed throughout the shipment process.
